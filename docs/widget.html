iPhone widget

Your WeatherBridge station on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, using the free Scriptable app.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free, by Simon B. Støvring).
  2. On this page, on the iPhone:
  3. Open Scriptable → + (top right) → paste → tap the title at the top and name it WeatherBridge → Done. Tap the script once to try it (it shows the medium widget).
  4. Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot → Edit → Add Widget → Scriptable → choose Small or Medium → Add Widget. Then touch and hold the new widget → Edit Widget → Script: WeatherBridge.
  5. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the widget area (rectangular) or the line above the clock (one line) → Scriptable → then tap it again and choose Script: WeatherBridge.
  6. Radar widgets (2.14): in the widget's settings (touch and hold → Edit Widget), type radar in the Parameter field. On the Lock Screen (rectangular) it shows the radar around homefor Michigan in white shades — iOS shows Lock Screen widgets without colour. On the Home Screen choose Small, Medium or Large (iPad: also Extra Large) for colour radar. Each picture shows its radar time.
  7. iPad Lock Screen, larger (2.14.1): turn the iPad sideways, touch and hold the Lock Screen → Customize → Lock Screen → Add Widgets on the left → Scriptable → the square size. Then choose Script WeatherBridge and type radar lock in the Parameter field.

The widget reads this public weather page over the internet (); nothing on your phone or network is shared with the station. iOS decides how often widgets refresh, typically every 15–60 minutes; tap the widget to open the full page.
